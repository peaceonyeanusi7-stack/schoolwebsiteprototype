# Legacy Academy Hub

Nursery, primary and secondary school build prompt 




Most premium private schools in Nigeria are Nursery, Primary & Secondary Schools operating under one brand. A combined website demonstrates a more sophisticated information architecture and is closer to what many real clients need.




I recommend replacing the separate primary and secondary demos with one flagship website.





---




AI Website Build Prompt – Premium Nursery, Primary & Secondary School




Create a premium, world-class, mobile-first website for a leading Nursery, Primary, and Secondary School in Nigeria.




The website should communicate academic excellence, child development, innovation, discipline, leadership, safety, creativity, and global education standards. It should immediately inspire confidence in parents while exciting students about learning and personal growth.




The design should feel prestigious, warm, modern, colorful, and highly organized. Every page should help parents understand the school's values, explore academic programmes, learn about admissions, and easily contact the school.




The school should appear comparable to the best private schools in Nigeria and internationally.





---




School Positioning




A premium private educational institution offering Nursery, Primary, and Secondary education under one unified learning community.




The school nurtures learners from their earliest years through adolescence by combining academic excellence, moral values, innovation, technology, leadership development, creativity, entrepreneurship, sports, and global citizenship.




The institution is committed to preparing confident, responsible, compassionate, and successful future leaders.





---




Unique Value Proposition




Provide a complete educational journey from early childhood through graduation in one trusted institution, offering highly qualified teachers, modern learning environments, personalized attention, technology-enabled education, leadership development, and strong partnerships with parents.




The website should clearly position the school as a lifelong learning community where every child is supported to discover their potential and achieve excellence.

Overall Design Direction




Create a premium, world-class educational website that reflects excellence from early childhood through secondary education. Use authentic photography, elegant typography, vibrant yet professional colours, generous white space, subtle animations, and intuitive navigation. Every page should reassure parents, inspire students, and present the school as a trusted institution committed to academic excellence, character development, innovation, safety, and preparing young people for lifelong success. The overall experience should feel like the digital presence of one of Nigeria's top private schools.
